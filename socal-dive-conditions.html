How this rating works — methodology, weights & limitations

Use judgment, not just numbers. This tool estimates dive quality from weather and wave models, not direct observation. It cannot see plankton blooms, red tides, local turbidity, or the actual surf hitting the beach. Always confirm with a live cam, a recent dive report, or your own eyes from shore — and dive within your training.

Wave & weather model data by Open-Meteo.com (CC BY 4.0). Tide predictions by NOAA CO-OPS (curve is cosine-interpolated between official highs/lows). Custom sites are saved on this device.
